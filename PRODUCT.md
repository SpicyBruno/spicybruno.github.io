# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential clients, collaborators and recruiters browsing the personal portfolio of Gabriele (studio name: **hwyl design**). They arrive from a link (CV, social, email), scan for taste and proof of work in under a minute, on desktop or phone. The job to be done: judge whether Gabriele's design sensibility fits their project and find how to contact him.

## Product Purpose

A one-page portfolio showcasing three projects (Sito Nonno / Carolus website, Finance Dashboard app, Calendario Editoriale template) across web design, UI and branding. The design itself is the primary proof of skill: the page must demonstrate craft, not just describe it. Success = visitor scrolls all three projects and clicks the contact CTA.

## Brand Personality

**Raw, bold, playful.** Street/graffiti energy: spray-paint display type, sticker badges, taped polaroids, film grain, imperfection as character. Loud but never sloppy; the chaos is art-directed. Italian-first voice, direct and warm ("Ciao, sono Gabriele!").

## Anti-references

- Corporate SaaS landing pages: gradient heroes, glassmorphism, pastel identical card grids.
- Editorial retro (cream/green serif) — explicitly discarded earlier in the project.
- Minimal white Helvetica portfolio galleries.

## Design Principles

1. **The page is the portfolio piece.** Every section must look art-directed, like a poster someone would photograph.
2. **Yellow is the voice, black is the room.** One committed accent (#F2B705 family) carries the identity; everything else stays near-monochrome.
3. **Analog artifacts, digital precision.** Tape, stickers, grain and rotation are hand-made gestures executed with pixel-exact code.
4. **Loud type, quiet body.** Graffiti/display faces only for shouts; reading text stays clean Space Grotesk with generous leading.
5. **Motion with swagger, never jank.** Springy reveals and parallax that respect prefers-reduced-motion and never animate layout properties.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA is non-negotiable (inherited from the studio's own design system): 4.5:1 body text, 3:1 large text/UI, visible focus rings on both themes, 44px touch targets, full keyboard navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored everywhere (loader skipped, shaders frozen, transforms suppressed). Bilingual IT/EN.
